# Career Workspace

A person's private working context for their career, including professional identity, aspirations, evidence and material prepared for different audiences.

## Language

**Career workspace**:
The private collection of career records and assets covering the person's professional life, aspirations, development and presentation.
_Avoid_: Profile (when referring to the whole workspace)

**Private garden**:
The inward-facing space for the person's aspirations, goals, personal objectives and internal reflection.
_Avoid_: Public profile

**Public face**:
The outward-facing presentation of the person, adapted to the audience or person receiving it and expressed through shared materials and online surfaces.
_Avoid_: Private profile, profile (without qualification)

**Career evidence**:
Experience, projects, feedback, certifications, repositories, experiments and published work that help establish what the person has done or demonstrated.
_Avoid_: Certifications only, externally verified proof only
