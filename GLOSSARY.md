# Career Workspace

A person's private working context for their career, including professional identity, aspirations, evidence and material prepared for different audiences.

## Language

**Career workspace**:
The entirely private collection of career records and assets covering the person's professional life, aspirations, projects, development and presentation, including material prepared for possible publication. Also called the private garden; nothing stored here is public merely because it is intended for a public audience.
_Avoid_: Profile (when referring to the whole workspace)

**Public face**:
The outward-facing presentation of the person, adapted to the audience or person receiving it and expressed through shared materials and online surfaces.
_Avoid_: Private profile, profile (without qualification)

**Career evidence**:
Material used to support what the person has done or demonstrated, such as repository work, results, feedback, credentials or published work. A project or experiment is not automatically evidence.
_Avoid_: Project (as an automatic synonym), certifications only, externally verified proof only

**Personal project**:
An undertaking outside the person's employment that can be tracked regardless of whether it is finished or publicly showcased. Its work and outcomes may provide career evidence.
_Avoid_: Evidence (when referring to the undertaking itself)

**Presentation**:
An expression of the person's professional identity for a particular surface or recipient, such as a website, LinkedIn profile or tailored resume.
_Avoid_: Separate identity

**Project record**:
The career-oriented account of a project: its purpose, status, skills used, personal contribution, learning and potential relevance to a resume or presentation, with links to the actual work.
_Avoid_: Project repository, automatic evidence

**Published surface**:
An external destination presenting career content, such as a LinkedIn account, personal website, GitHub profile or credential service. Its current content establishes what is actually published there.
_Avoid_: Final directory, local approved copy (as proof of publication)

**Published state**:
The content actually present on a particular published surface at a given time. It may differ from drafts or previously approved material in the private career workspace.
_Avoid_: Approved draft, intended content

**Staging**:
The private collection of the latest approved material intended for publication or sharing, including reusable content and destination-specific adaptations. Its presence does not establish that publication occurred.
_Avoid_: Published state, unreviewed draft

**Reusable content**:
Approved wording or an asset that can serve multiple presentations without maintaining independent copies. A surface-specific adaptation expresses an intentional difference from that shared material.
_Avoid_: One separate master per surface

**Reconciliation**:
A user-directed decision about differences between approved staged material and observed published content, including whether to retain, import or publish a change. A difference alone does not establish which version is correct.
_Avoid_: Automatic overwrite, unconditional import of public claims as facts

**Publication area**:
The private part of the career workspace containing material intended for external publication or sharing, including drafts and approved staged material. Its contents are not evidence of actual publication.
_Avoid_: Public directory, final directory

**Publication draft**:
A proposed text or asset intended for external use that has not yet been approved for staging.
_Avoid_: Staged material, published content

**Content discrepancy**:
A difference between workspace material and observed content on an external surface that requires the user's choice to reconcile. It does not by itself identify which side changed or which should prevail.
_Avoid_: Automatically incorrect public content

**Core profile**:
The private account of who the person is professionally, what they stand for and what they want from their career. It includes supported experience alongside clearly distinguished aspirations, goals and preferences.
_Avoid_: Public profile, LinkedIn profile, facts-only profile

**Personal brand**:
The guidance for how the person wants to be perceived and addressed: positioning, intended audiences, target clients or employers, messaging, and visual identity rules. It is grounded in the core profile and distinct from the reusable outputs prepared for external use.
_Avoid_: Published content, online presence

**Online presence**:
The career-related content and surfaces through which the person is represented online. It describes what is externally presented, rather than the person's private profile or intended positioning.
_Avoid_: Core profile, intended personal brand

**Reputation**:
How other people perceive the person professionally, reflected imperfectly in feedback and other attributed signals. It is distinct from intended positioning and observable published content.
_Avoid_: Online presence, verified audience perception (without evidence)

**Career assets**:
Reusable material prepared from the profile and personal-brand guidance for external presentation, including bios, headlines, resumes and visual assets. Drafts and approved staged versions remain private within the workspace.
_Avoid_: Personal-brand guidance, actual published state

**Surface record**:
The workspace description of an external career surface: its service, account or location, intended use and how its content can be inspected or updated. It can reference reusable career assets without duplicating their content.
_Avoid_: Copy of every published page

**Career research**:
User-selected useful findings about companies, opportunities, comparable professionals, resumes or other career-relevant subjects, with sources, observations, uncertainties and their relevance to the person. Research is retained selectively in the private career workspace, not as an archive of everything discovered.
_Avoid_: Personal-brand audience definition, verified fact without supporting sources

**Comparator**:
An external professional profile, resume or portfolio selected as a relevant reference for understanding the market or improving the person's presentation, recorded as links and useful observations rather than a locally copied resume. It may be a peer or aspirational example; comparison does not establish that its author competes for the same vacancy.
_Avoid_: Confirmed competitor, model to copy

**Research selection**:
The user's choice of which shortlisted findings to retain in the career workspace, whether some, all or none. Discovery alone does not imply selection.
_Avoid_: Automatic retention of every search result

**Research archive**:
Retained research removed from active consideration while preserving its reference value and history. Archiving follows the user's request or established guidance; noticing stale content alone does not authorize a change.
_Avoid_: Deletion, expired-equals-useless
