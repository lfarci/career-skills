# Review Research

## Contract
- **Input:** User-selected research/ scope, saved references and retention/archival guidance.
- **Scope:** Review on request; archive under guidance, leave deletion manual and preserve useful historical references.
- **Output:** Updated research/ records and authorized moves to research/archive/ with repaired links, reasons/dates and a change summary.

## Review
- Read the workspace index, selected research records and the user's retention guidance. Clarify only missing scope that affects the review; do not require another skill, provider or a fixed folder layout.
- Assess usefulness for future reference, market understanding, resume inspiration and targeting. Consider why the user saved a record. Expired opportunities or older profiles can remain useful; age alone is not a reason to archive.
- Identify outdated assertions, dead links, duplication and reduced relevance. Verify current facts only when needed and accessible; distinguish observed problems from unknowns. Research about others never establishes facts about the user.

## Propose and apply
- Present concise keep, refresh or archive recommendations with reasons. Follow explicit record/batch selections or established user guidance; ask about ambiguous cases. No automatic deletion: deletion remains manual.
- Archive authorized entries within the existing research conventions, preserving source links, history and reason/date. Repair affected internal links; avoid copying an active record into two competing canonical locations. Confirm actual changes.
- When appropriate, refresh useful observations from inspected sources; keep old dates/status explicit. Do not download comparator resumes, create new unrelated research or publish anything.
- Run only when requested. Other skills encountering stale research should ask whether a review is wanted rather than silently triggering this workflow or modifying records.

## Done when
- Retained/refreshed/archived records follow user guidance and their reference value is considered.
- Archive actions and link checks are reported accurately; no records were automatically deleted.
- Access limitations and unresolved choices remain explicit.
