# Define Design Brief

## Contract
- **Input:** The user’s current design request, explicitly selected references and relevant brand/ guidance.
- **Scope:** Interview about visual purpose and deliverables; do not inherit historical aesthetics or produce assets.
- **Output:** brand/discovery.md (or mapped brief): substantive answers, scope, intended impression, constraints and unresolved choices.

## Interview
- Ask one high-level question at a time. Start with what the user wants to create or improve and why; wait before suggesting deliverables, fonts or styles. If the current request already answers this, ask the next missing question.
- Explore purpose, professional context/domain, audience, where/how the design will be used, and what it should make people feel, associate or remember. Probe vague answers and conflicting needs like a design-focused grill-me interview; do not require that skill.
- Do not infer a tech aesthetic from a technical career or import past experiments and website styling. Reuse choices only when the user explicitly supplies a reference as the basis or asks to continue a brief. A link to this skill is not a design reference. Clarify which qualities to retain.
- After understanding the need, guide unresolved choices with a few relevant examples and trade-offs. Discuss personality/style and logo type only when relevant. Recommend from the answers, not before them.
- Agree on deliverables and priorities: logo, typography/composition, palette, banners, icons or a broader identity as needed. Confirm wording, constraints, freedom to change and target formats/sizes. Keep scope career-facing: professional profiles, resumes, portfolios, talks and related personal projects.

## Record and hand off
- Ask where to save unless established. Reuse canonical records; save substantive questions/answers and decisions in discovery.md, and summarize the actionable brief there. Distinguish current answers, interpretations, references and superseded choices.
- Include purpose, audience, usage, desired impression, agreed scope, constraints, reference links and unresolved questions. Let the user correct the summary. Do not invent answers or generate assets.
- Offer creation as the next stage. create-design-assets may use this brief if installed, but the document must stand alone. Leave visual concept research and selection to creation; do not force users through the interview again.

When the workspace has a root `README.md` index, add a link to each new record under `Records`.

## Done when
- The brief supports concrete design decisions and the requested deliverables are explicit.
- Answers and unresolved choices are recorded at the agreed destination, or supplied as a reviewable handoff when saving is unavailable.
- No unrequested historical direction or assets have been introduced.
