# Personal Brand

Define *how* professional facts and aspirations are communicated to different audiences. Do not rewrite source facts to match a preferred slogan.

## Steps

1. Review verified professional history, user's intended career direction and constraints. Separate existing strengths from aspirational capabilities.
2. Identify important audiences (recruiters, hiring managers, technical peers, collaborators) and surfaces already used or planned.
3. Draft a concise core positioning and evidence-backed differentiators. Explain any proposed change to established messaging; surface competing positioning choices for human judgment.
4. Define the distinct purpose of each surface. Different headlines can be appropriate where they communicate consistent underlying facts.
5. Determine which assets merit an individual audit or a full cross-asset alignment audit. Use `audit-surface` and `audit` when available, but remain independently functional if they are not installed.
6. Produce prioritized recommendations or reviewable proposed edits. Never claim a platform has changed until the modification is confirmed, and ask before public publishing.

## Output: Professional Brand Plan

- Core identity and positioning choices
- Intended audiences and evidence-backed differentiators
- Surface inventory and individual purpose
- Content/message guidance for each relevant surface
- Prioritized actions, dependencies and approval needs

## Done when

- Positioning is coherent with verified career facts and selected goals.
- Legitimate tailoring is not misidentified as inconsistency.
- Each recommendation names a target surface and expected outcome.
- Plans/experiments are not presented as accomplished production work.
- No unauthorized public changes occur.
