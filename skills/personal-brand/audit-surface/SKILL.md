# Audit One Professional Surface

Verify an individual professional asset against available evidence and its intended audience. This is not a full SEO, accessibility, performance or security audit.

## Record ownership
Use profile/ for supported personal facts and direction, projects/ for project accounts, and brand/ for positioning, audience and design guidance. Put reusable external-facing outputs in assets/: drafts until approved, staged after explicit approval, with shared content reused across surfaces. Read services.md or its mapped equivalent for surface details. Actual published content lives externally; differences require the user's reconciliation choice and factual conflicts must be flagged before importing. Existing mapped paths take precedence; do not migrate files just to match these names.

## Workspace
Read the chosen career workspace index and relevant canonical records when available. Follow its source map and privacy/write boundaries; link evidence and outputs instead of duplicating them. Without an index, use supplied inputs and ask only task-relevant gaps. Work with Markdown wherever stored; do not require setup, a provider or folder migration.

## Steps

1. Identify the exact asset, accessible version, date checked and intended audience. State which sections cannot be accessed; accept pasted or exported content.
2. Extract observable claims and evaluate against available canonical records. Distinguish **confirmed mismatch**, **potential issue needing verification**, and **stylistic opportunity**.
3. Inspect relevant surface quality: accuracy of titles/dates/credentials, links, audience fit, clarity, recency and unsupported project maturity or impact. **When visual assets are observable**, compare the actual banner, logo/avatar, icons, colors and typography with any approved brand kit. Check obvious crop/legibility problems while allowing platform-specific sizes and font fallbacks. Assess only what can actually be observed.
4. Consolidate duplicate findings and prioritize by risk and user impact. For every actionable issue, give current state, supporting evidence, desired outcome and a verifiable acceptance criterion.
5. Produce a report. When tracker access and authorization exist, first check existing issues; otherwise output issue-ready text. Do not edit the public asset or create tickets silently.

## Output: Surface Audit Report

- Asset, source/version, checked date and access limits
- Findings with category, confidence, evidence and priority
- Specific suggested corrections, including visual-brand drift when evidence permits
- De-duplicated actionable items with acceptance criteria

## Done when

- Findings are grounded in inspected content; inaccessible content is not guessed.
- Confirmed contradictions cite relevant evidence.
- Copy variation and appropriate graphic sizing are distinguished from factual or meaningful visual-brand drift.
- Missing access or absent visual guidelines is reported as unknown, not a made-up inconsistency.
- Every high-priority finding identifies a measurable correction.
- No duplicate tracker issues or claimed unperformed updates.
