# LinkedIn

Patterns, not text to copy. Fill every slot from the profile records; mark gaps `[CONFIRM]`.

## Limits (approximate, checked 2026-10)
- Headline: 220 characters; search and mobile show about the first 60, so lead with the role.
- About: 2,600 characters; about 220 show before "see more", so the first two lines carry the pitch.
- Experience description: 2,000 characters per role.

## Headline patterns
- **Employed senior engineer:** `<Role> · <core stack or domain> · <one proof or focus>`
  e.g. `Senior Backend Engineer · .NET & Azure · payments platforms at scale`
- **Freelance / consultant:** `<Role> for <client type> · <outcome you deliver> · <availability or location>`
  e.g. `Freelance Cloud Engineer for SMEs · Azure migrations that cut run costs · Brussels / remote`
- **Career changer / junior:** `<Target role> · <relevant new skill> · <transferable strength from before>`
  e.g. `Junior Data Analyst · SQL & Python · 6 years of retail operations behind the numbers`

## About patterns
1. Hook (1–2 lines): who you help and how, in the target role's words.
2. Proof (2–4 bullets or short lines): confirmed results, projects, certifications.
3. How you work / what you care about (1–2 lines, optional).
4. Call to action: what to contact you about, and how.

Variants:
- **Employee:** proof = scope, systems, team impact; CTA optional ("Happy to talk about …").
- **Consultant:** proof = client outcomes (anonymise if needed); CTA explicit (services, availability).
- **Career changer:** one line on the switch, then new-skill proof (projects, courses), then transferable strengths; no apology.

## Avoid
Buzzword strings, claims not in the records, private plans, a title separated from its employer and dates.
