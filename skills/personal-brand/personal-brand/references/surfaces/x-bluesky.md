# X and Bluesky

Patterns, not text to copy. Short bios: one identity line, one proof or focus, one link.

## Limits (approximate, checked 2026-10)
- X: bio 160 characters, name 50.
- Bluesky: bio 256 characters (graphemes), display name 64.

## Patterns
- **Engineer:** `<Role> · <stack/domain>. Writing about <topic>. <City>`
  e.g. `Backend engineer · .NET & Azure. Notes on cloud costs and APIs. Brussels`
- **Consultant:** `<Role> helping <client type> <outcome>. <link to services>`
- **Career changer:** `<Old field> → <new role>. Learning <skill> in public.`

Keep the same role words as LinkedIn so people recognise you across surfaces.
