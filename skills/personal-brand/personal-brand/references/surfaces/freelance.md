# Malt and other freelance platforms

Patterns, not text to copy. Clients scan for service, stack, sector and availability. Platform limits change and are not published consistently: keep the title short (about 60–80 characters) and check the field counter on the platform.

## Title
`<Role> <main stack> · <service or sector>` e.g. `Azure Cloud Engineer · migrations & DevOps for SMEs`

## Description (300–1,000 characters)
1. What you deliver and for whom (1–2 sentences).
2. Three or four services or typical missions.
3. Confirmed proof: past missions (anonymised if needed), certifications.
4. How you work: remote/on-site, languages, availability (only what the user confirms).

Rates, availability and client names are `[CONFIRM]` until the user states them.
