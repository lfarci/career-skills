# Personal website

Patterns, not text to copy. No platform limit; aim for a hero readable in five seconds.

## Hero
- Line 1 (name + role): `<Name>, <Role>`
- Line 2 (value, ≤ 20 words): `I <build/help> <what> for <whom>.`
- One primary call to action: `See my work` / `Get in touch` / `Download resume`.

## About (120–250 words)
1. Who you are and what you do now.
2. Two or three confirmed highlights (projects, results, certifications).
3. How you work or what you are looking for (only if public).
4. Contact line.

Variants: employee (lead with craft and recent work), consultant (lead with services and outcomes, add a short services list), career changer (lead with the new direction and project proof).
