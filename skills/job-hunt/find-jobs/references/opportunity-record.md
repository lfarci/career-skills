# Opportunity record

Save to `research/opportunities/<company-slug>-<role-slug>.md`. Header fields are fixed; sections below are free but keep this order when used.

```markdown
# <Role title>: <Company name>

Company: [<Company name>](../companies/<company-slug>.md)   (plain name when no company record exists)
Status: <open | closed | unverified>
Checked: <YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MMZ>
Source: <employer or authorized ATS URL>
Apply: <application URL>

## Requirements
## Strong matches
## Gaps and risks
## Hard-constraint check
## Fit rationale
```

Requirements and company facts stay in their own record; link instead of copying the company description.
