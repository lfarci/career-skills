# Standard workspace initialization reference

**Managed destination:** A private `career/` with `README.md` as its index and five standardized homes: `profile/` (facts, direction, development), `projects/` (work and contributions), `brand/` (positioning and design guidance), `assets/` (reusable drafts, approved staged assets and service descriptions), and `research/` (selected opportunities, companies and comparators). For a complete managed setup, scaffold all five homes with a brief `README.md` in each (purpose and whether content is pending); Git cannot track empty directories. Only create substantive records when selected, and do not fabricate content or filler nested directories. User-chosen Markdown-capable storage and optional PR workflows are supported.

**Existing documents are sources:** User-selected CVs, old notes, project files, credential records and exported web profiles may feed the new canonical records. Inventory first; ask what to use; extract selected claims into the matching home with source location/date and uncertainty. Reconcile conflicts with the user. Preserve originals and avoid copying whole historical trees or creating two canonical copies.

**Existing occupied `career/`:** A fresh `career/` needs a different private location; otherwise propose an **explicit in-place migration** with a path/link/collision plan. Never overwrite or move existing records just because the target names differ. When structural repair is required, `validate-workspace` may assist if installed, but setup remains independently usable.

**Index and lifecycle:** `career/README.md` records the source map (including pending/unselected sources), permissions, canonical owners, optional tracker and publication boundaries. `assets/drafts/` and `assets/staged/` are not published state; `assets/services.md` (when needed) maps destinations. Research is user-selected and archived under guidance, not automatically deleted. Evidence, plans, public copy and published state remain distinct. Never store secrets or private career content in the public skill repository.

**Role vs engagement import:** `profile/experience.md` owns employers, grades/functions, dates and user-confirmed internships. Detailed consulting assignments, internship projects, contribution evidence and outcomes belong in `projects/` and may be linked from the role record. Do not silently omit roles found in selected sources; ask about apparent conflicts or missing periods. Verify that the resulting README/source status differentiates reviewed and still-pending inputs.

**After renaming a tracked legacy root:** Git may report `D career/...` and `?? career-old/` until paths are staged; editor source-control views may still show deleted old paths. Check actual working directories and file contents before claiming the new `career/` includes old names. Keep the old root as a source until separately approved for removal, and validate the new root's five homes.
