# Review Progress

## Contract
- **Input:** profile/development.md, the review period and actual activity/outcome evidence.
- **Scope:** Assess and revise development commitments; propose factual updates separately rather than publishing achievements.
- **Output:** The same development plan and dated reviews: progress, blockers, justified adjustments and next actions.

## Review the evidence
- Read the current plan, goals, previous review and available outcomes. Establish the review period and ask only for missing evidence that changes the assessment. Remain useful with a pasted plan; do not require another skill or tracker.
- Compare intended outcomes with what was done and demonstrated. Distinguish reported activity, completed learning, applied capability and externally corroborated results. Missing evidence means unknown, not failure or completion.
- Explore blockers, changed priorities and feasibility without treating missed goals as personal shortcomings. Consider whether the plan or direction needs adjustment rather than merely increasing effort.

## Adjust and record
- Summarize progress, useful evidence, unresolved gaps and a few next steps. Explain changes to priorities, scope or cadence; preserve previous decisions and label superseded actions. Avoid invented percentage-complete or competence scores.
- Ask where to save unless established; update the canonical private plan/review rather than duplicate it. Link completed evidence to the user's professional record, proposing or applying factual updates only within authorization. Plans and partial work must not become public achievement claims.
- Recommend revisiting direction if goals no longer fit. Do not require plan-development, reflect or maintain-profile to be installed; provide a standalone review and actionable adjustments.
- Do not schedule recurring reviews, enroll in training or publish profile changes unless requested.

## Done when
- Progress is tied to specific evidence and the review period, with unknowns explicit.
- Changes and next actions are justified, feasible and traceable to the plan.
- Private records stay private and actual save/update status is clear.
